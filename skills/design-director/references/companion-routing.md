# Companion skill routing

Design Director remains the single director. Companion skills provide specialist knowledge or an additional implementation/audit lens.

## frontend-design
Use when:
- creating a new visually important surface;
- major redesign needs stronger art direction;
- implementation should be distinctive rather than template-like.

Give it the Design Director thesis/invariants. Do not let it replace scope or rewrite product requirements.

## ui-ux-pro-max
Use for:
- product-pattern reference;
- palette/type alternatives;
- charts/mobile/product-type patterns;
- framework-specific UI knowledge.

Treat it as a library, not an automatic aesthetic authority.

## popular-web-designs
Use when a concrete reference language materially helps. Extract principles such as density, typography, navigation, proof, spacing, or interaction. Do not clone brand identity or copy.

## web-design-guidelines
Use near final review for systematic interface/accessibility checks. Feed important findings back into Design Director for prioritization rather than applying every lint-like suggestion blindly.

## react-best-practices
Use when React/Next architecture, rendering, loading, bundle/performance, or component patterns are relevant to the user-visible quality.

## claude-design (optional)
Can be used as a second-opinion lens when installed, but avoid parallel redesign directions. Design Director resolves conflicts.

## Conflict policy

When specialist advice conflicts:
1. explicit user constraints;
2. product truth and task completion;
3. accessibility/safety;
4. current design system/project memory;
5. Design Director thesis;
6. specialist heuristic.

Never invoke companions merely to prove they were installed.

## Revenue-Centric Design companion (optional, separately licensed)

If `revenue-centric-design` is installed, it can serve as a specialist for SaaS/startup CRO, onboarding/activation, churn/retention, pricing/monetization, feature discipline, positioning, and growth questions.

Use it when:
- the user explicitly asks for conversion/revenue/activation/retention optimization;
- a landing page/pricing/onboarding problem depends on business mechanics beyond visual craft;
- the Design Director outcome layer identifies a growth/product-strategy question that needs deeper specialist coverage.

Do not treat it as the visual director. Design Director still owns composition, UI quality, accessibility, scope, implementation, and verification.
