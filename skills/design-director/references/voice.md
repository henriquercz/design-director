# Brand voice and art direction

Use `voice` on marketing, launch, campaign, editorial, portfolio, and other surfaces where recognition and emotional posture are part of the deliverable.

## Start with visitor job

Brand work still serves a task. Determine what the visitor must believe, understand, compare, remember, or do.

## Build from the prompt invariants

Use exact identity, category, user pressure, core artifact, and proof. The strongest brand detail is often a specific real object from the domain rather than decorative abstraction.

## Lane sentence

Write a one-sentence visual lane internally, e.g. "quiet archival precision around the contract itself" or "kinetic match-day energy organized around live fixtures." It should be specific enough to reject generic alternatives.

## Physical association method

For type/image/material direction, ask what physical objects, places, media, or instruments belong to this domain. Use those associations to inform texture, scale, framing, typography, and imagery without literal cosplay.

## Evidence before ornament

If trust is the task, lead with real proof: product artifact, result, demonstration, customer outcome, process, comparison, or specific story.

## Imagery

Prefer subject-specific images/illustration/product artifacts. Avoid stock visuals that could market ten unrelated companies.

## Variation with coherence

Sections may change rhythm, background, image treatment, or scale, but should share recognizable DNA through type, spacing, edge logic, color relationships, or repeated motifs.

## Voice bar

A voice pass must make the rendered surface more identifiable with this brand/product. A new accent color alone is not art direction.
